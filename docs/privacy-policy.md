# LDR Companion Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 14, 2026

**Status:** Pre-release draft; operator details must be completed before publication.

LDR Companion is a private, two-person companion app for partners who choose to
connect their accounts. This policy explains what information the app processes,
why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

## Operator and contact

LDR Companion is currently operated by the developer identified in its App Store
listing and repository.

**Before release, replace this paragraph with the operator's legal name, business
or mailing address, country, and a monitored privacy/support email address.** Until
then, privacy and deletion questions can be submitted through the
[LDR Companion repository](https://github.com/zgoldwyn/ldr-games/issues). Do not
include private account or relationship information in a public issue.

## Information we process

- **Account information:** email address, authentication credentials in hashed
  form, internal account identifiers, and technical session information used to
  secure the account.
- **Relationship content:** pairing status, game sessions and moves, and—when
  those features are enabled—quiz responses, relationship dates, reminders, and
  drawings or images you choose to create or upload.
- **Notification information:** notification preferences, delivery state, and an
  optional device push token after you enable push notifications.
- **Operational information:** timestamps and limited infrastructure records
  needed to synchronize data, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and enforce one
  active session per account.

The current app does not collect names, postal addresses, phone numbers,
contacts, precise location, health or financial data, advertising identifiers,
or application-level analytics. It does not sell or share personal information
for cross-context behavioral advertising and does not serve advertising.

## How information is collected and used

Information is collected when you create or use an account, pair with a partner,
play a game, enable notifications, or submit content. It is used only to provide
and secure the app: authenticate you, connect you with the partner you select,
synchronize shared content, run shared features, deliver requested notifications,
prevent abuse, and maintain reliability.

For users where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, account and shared-feature processing
is necessary to perform the service you request; security and limited operational
logging support the operator's legitimate interests in protecting the service;
optional push notifications are enabled only at your request. You can withdraw
notification permission in device settings without losing core app access.

## Who can see shared content

Content belonging to a pairing is available only to the two accounts in that
active pairing. Some games intentionally withhold hidden information until the
rules reveal it. When a pairing ends, access to pairing-owned content is revoked.

## Service providers and transfers

LDR Companion uses processors that handle information on its behalf:

- [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) provides authentication, database,
  file storage, realtime synchronization, infrastructure logging, and server
  functions.
- Apple provides App Store distribution and Apple Push Notification service on
  iOS, subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Push messages use generic wording; full private content remains inside the
authenticated app. These providers may process information in countries other
than your own. Before offering the app in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the
operator must document the hosting region, processor terms, and an applicable
international-transfer mechanism. Providers are not authorized by LDR Companion
to use app data for their own advertising.

## Cookies, analytics, and tracking

The native app does not use browser cookies, advertising SDKs, third-party
analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or third-party embeds. It stores only
the local session, cached app state, and theme preference needed to provide the
service. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals therefore do not
change current app behavior because the app does not perform cross-site tracking
or sell/share personal information.

See the [Cookie and Similar Technologies Policy](cookie-policy.md). If optional
analytics, embeds, or non-essential storage are added, they must be documented
and kept disabled until any legally required consent is obtained.

## Retention and deletion

Undelivered notifications are retained for no more than 30 days. Authentication
failure and security records are retained only as needed to operate lockout and
abuse-prevention controls. Other account and relationship information is retained
while the account is active unless a shorter period is stated for a feature.

You can permanently delete your account from **Settings → Delete account**. The
app requires a second confirmation and explains that deletion is irreversible.
Confirmed deletion removes credentials, account-owned information, notifications,
and content owned by pairings the account belonged to. It also ends an active
pairing and leaves the other partner unpaired. Encrypted service-provider backups
may age out according to the provider's backup-retention process and are not used
to restore a deleted account.

## Your choices and rights

You can:

- decline or disable push notifications in device settings;
- end a pairing without deleting your individual account;
- delete your account and associated content in the app; and
- request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or an objection
  where applicable law provides that right.

Before release, the operator must publish a private contact channel and a process
for verifying and answering rights requests. If the CCPA applies to the operator
in the future, the service must also provide the notices and request methods that
law requires. The service currently does not sell or share personal information.

## Security

The app uses encrypted network connections, access controls scoped to the signed-
in account and active pairing, server-side authorization checks, private storage,
and session revocation. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Children

LDR Companion is not directed to children under 13, and the operator does not
knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The launch plan
must set an appropriate App Store age rating and decide whether to require users
to be at least 18. If the operator learns that a child under 13 submitted personal
information, it will delete that information.

## Changes

Material changes will be described by updating the effective date and, when
appropriate, providing an in-app notice. Consent will be requested again when a
change introduces processing that legally requires new consent.
