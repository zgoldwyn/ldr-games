# LDR Companion Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 8, 2026

LDR Companion is a private, two-person companion app for partners who choose to
connect their accounts. This policy explains what information the app processes,
why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

## Information we process

- **Account information:** your email address, authentication credentials in
  hashed form, and technical session information used to keep your account
  secure.
- **Relationship content:** pairing status, game sessions and moves, quiz
  responses, relationship dates, reminders, and images you choose to create or
  upload.
- **Notification information:** notification preferences, delivery state, and an
  optional device push token.
- **Operational information:** timestamps and limited technical records needed
  to synchronize data, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and enforce one active
  session per account.

We do not sell personal information, serve advertising, or use third-party
advertising trackers.

## How information is used

Information is used only to provide and secure the app: authenticate you, connect
you with the partner you select, synchronize shared content, run shared games and
relationship features, deliver notifications, and maintain reliability.

Content belonging to a pairing is visible only to the two accounts in that active
pairing. When a pairing ends, access to pairing-owned content is revoked.

## Service providers

LDR Companion uses service providers that process information on our behalf:

- [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) provides authentication, database,
  file storage, realtime synchronization, and server functions.
- Apple provides App Store distribution and Apple Push Notification service on
  iOS, subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Push messages use generic wording; full private content remains inside the
authenticated app.

We do not authorize these providers to use your information for their own
advertising.

## Retention and deletion

Undelivered notifications are retained for no more than 30 days. Other account
and relationship information is retained while your account is active unless a
shorter period is required for a particular feature.

You can permanently delete your account from **Settings → Delete account**. The
app requires a second confirmation and explains that deletion is irreversible.
Confirmed deletion removes your credentials, account-owned information,
notifications, and content owned by any pairing you belonged to. It also ends
the pairing and leaves the other partner unpaired. Service-provider backups may
age out according to the provider's secure backup-retention process.

## Security

The app uses encrypted network connections, access controls scoped to the signed-
in account and active pairing, server-side authorization checks, and session
revocation. No system can guarantee absolute security, but access is limited to
what is needed to operate the service.

## Children

LDR Companion is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected on this page by updating the effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or deletion help, open a private support request through
the app when that option is available. Until then, contact the developer through
the [LDR Companion repository](https://github.com/zgoldwyn/ldr-games/issues).
