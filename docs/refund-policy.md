# LDR Companion Refund Policy

**Effective date:** September 14, 2026

LDR Companion currently has no paid purchases, subscriptions, or direct payment
collection, so there is currently nothing to refund.

If App Store purchases or subscriptions are added, the app must show the price,
billing period, automatic-renewal terms, cancellation method, and material limits
before purchase. Purchases processed by Apple are subject to Apple's billing and
refund processes and applicable consumer law. Users can request an Apple refund
at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) and manage Apple
subscriptions through their App Store account settings.

Deleting an LDR Companion account does not automatically cancel an App Store
subscription. If subscriptions are introduced, the deletion flow must explain
this and link directly to subscription management before deletion.

Nothing in this policy limits refund, cancellation, cooling-off, or other rights
that cannot be waived under applicable law. Before any paid launch, this policy
must be updated for the actual seller, product, territories, and payment channel
and reviewed by counsel.
